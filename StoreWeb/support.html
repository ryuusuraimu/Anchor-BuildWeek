Anchor

Support

Help for the first public release of Anchor.

Anchor is a preparation and communication aid for moments when speaking or explaining may be hard. It is not a medical device, treatment tool, emergency service, or substitute for professional care.

Common Steps

  • Use Studio to edit your Shield card before you need it.
  • Add a support contact manually or from Contacts.
  • Use the QR view to show support instructions without internet.
  • Use Journal to reflect privately after a hard moment.

Privacy

Anchor stores support cards, journal entries, and support contact information locally on your device in the first public release.

Urgent Help

If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or someone nearby.

Contact

For support, use the support address listed on Anchor's App Store page.
