# Anchor Age Rating Answers

Last updated: 2026-07-08

## Recommendation

Anchor v1 should be suitable for a broad audience, but because it references panic, overwhelm, safety notes, and support during hard moments, avoid presenting it as a children's app.

Expected App Store age rating outcome should likely remain low, but final rating must be produced by App Store Connect's questionnaire.

## Content Answers For v1

Use these answers unless the app changes before submission:

- Cartoon or fantasy violence: None
- Realistic violence: None
- Sexual content or nudity: None
- Profanity or crude humor: None
- Alcohol, tobacco, or drug references: None
- Mature or suggestive themes: None
- Horror/fear themes: None
- Medical/treatment information: None
- Gambling: None
- Unrestricted web access: No
- User-generated public content: No
- Messaging between users: No
- Location sharing: No
- Purchases: No for v1, unless monetization is added before submission
- Advertising: No for v1

## Sensitive Theme Note

Anchor mentions panic, overwhelm, breathing, journaling, support contact, and safety notes. These are framed as preparation and communication support, not medical treatment or emergency response.

If App Store Connect asks whether the app includes medical/treatment information, answer based on the final wording shown in the questionnaire. For v1, Anchor should not be presented as providing medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical content.

## Review Consistency

The App Review notes, Privacy Policy, metadata, and in-app Settings all state that Anchor is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, treatment tool, emergency service, or substitute for professional care.
