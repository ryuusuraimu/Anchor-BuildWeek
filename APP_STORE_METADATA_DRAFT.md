# Anchor App Store Metadata Draft

Last updated: 2026-07-08

## Working Positioning

Anchor is a calm preparation app for panic, anxiety, and overwhelm moments when speaking, deciding, or explaining may become difficult. It helps users prepare a simple support card, trusted contact, QR message, breathing practice, learning content, and private reflection journal.

Anchor should not be positioned as a medical, diagnostic, treatment, monitoring, emergency response, or guaranteed safety product.

## Description

Anchor helps you prepare before a panic or overwhelm moment, so you do not have to explain everything from scratch when words are hard.

Create a simple Shield card with:

- What is happening
- What helps
- What to avoid
- Important safety notes
- A trusted support contact

You can show the card on screen, share it through a QR code, or keep it available from the Lock Screen while Shield is active. Anchor also includes guided breathing, private journaling, and short learning cards for people who want to support someone calmly.

Anchor is designed around preparation, low-friction access, and calm communication. It is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, treatment tool, emergency service, or substitute for professional care. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or someone nearby.

## App Review Notes Draft

Anchor is a personal preparation and communication aid. It lets users prepare a short support card and QR message that can be shown to someone nearby when explaining their needs is difficult.

Anchor does not diagnose, treat, monitor, measure, or replace emergency services. It does not provide medical advice. If a user is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or a trusted person nearby.

Contacts access is optional. It is used only when the user chooses a trusted support contact. The selected contact is stored locally on device.

Journal entries and Shield card content are stored locally on device. Anchor does not require an account and does not send this information to a server.

While Shield is active, Anchor uses audio background mode / Now Playing so the prepared Lock Screen card can remain visible and navigable from the Lock Screen. The audio is not user-facing content; it supports the Lock Screen card experience.

Suggested review path:

1. Launch Anchor.
2. Complete or skip the setup flow.
3. Open Studio and edit the Shield card.
4. Add a contact manually or through Contacts permission.
5. Tap Create Shield or Deploy Shield from Home.
6. Tap the QR button to view the QR card.
7. On a real iPhone, lock the phone while Shield is active to review the Lock Screen card.
