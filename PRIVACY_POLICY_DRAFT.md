# Anchor Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-07-18

This draft is written for the first public release of Anchor. Review it before publishing, and update any future data practices if the app changes.

## Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Anchor is designed as a personal preparation and communication aid. This Privacy Policy explains how Anchor handles information on your device.

## Information You Enter

Anchor may let you enter:

- Support card text, such as what is happening, what helps, what to avoid, and safety notes
- Trusted support relay contacts
- Journal entries and mood selections
- App preferences, such as theme and Shield settings

This information is stored locally on your device, except for the limited voice-generation flow described below.

## Optional AI-Generated Voice

If you choose **Preview** in Voice & reading, Anchor sends only a built-in sample sentence to an Anchor voice proxy and OpenAI's speech API. If you choose **Create offline reading**, Anchor sends your prepared Shield wording. The purpose is only to create the audio you requested. Voice generation does not run automatically and does not run from the Shield screen.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in. The speech endpoint may retain abuse-monitoring logs containing customer content for up to 30 days under default API data controls. The production voice proxy must be deployed without request-body logging and with appropriate security controls before this feature ships.

The generated AAC audio is saved locally on your device so Shield can read it without a network connection. If no generated file is available, Shield uses the iOS device voice instead.

## Contacts

Anchor may ask for access to your contacts if you choose to select trusted support contacts from your address book.

Contacts access is optional. Anchor uses it only to let you choose a contact and phone number. The selected contact information is saved locally on your device. Anchor does not upload your contacts to a server.

You can also add a contact manually without granting Contacts access.

## Journal Entries

Journal entries are private to your device. Anchor does not send journal entries to a server.

## QR Codes and Sharing

Anchor can generate QR codes from the support card text you prepare. Anyone who scans the QR code may be able to read the information included in it. Only include information you are comfortable showing to someone nearby.

If you use system sharing features, the information you choose to share may be handled by the app or service you share it with.

## Background Audio / Lock Screen Card

When Shield is active, Anchor may use the iOS audio / Now Playing system so the prepared card can remain visible from the Lock Screen. This is used to support the Lock Screen card experience.

## Data Collection

Anchor does not require an account. Contacts and journal entries are not sent to the voice service. Prepared Shield wording is transmitted only when you explicitly request an offline reading; Preview uses fixed sample text.

The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the current version.

## Data Storage and Deletion

Anchor stores app data locally on your device using iOS storage. You can delete or edit entries and contact information inside the app where those controls are available.

You can also clear local Anchor data from Settings. This removes the Shield card, journal entries, support relay contacts, generated voice audio, saved versions, and preferences stored by Anchor on that device.

You can also delete Anchor from your device to remove locally stored app data associated with the app.

## Safety and Health Notice

Anchor is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, treatment tool, emergency service, or substitute for professional care. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a trusted person nearby.

## Children's Privacy

Anchor is not directed to children under 13. If the final launch plan targets younger users or schools, this policy and the app experience should be reviewed and updated before release.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Anchor changes. The effective date will be updated when the policy changes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support address listed on Anchor's App Store page.
