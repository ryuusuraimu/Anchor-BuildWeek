# Anchor Ads Launch Plan

Last updated: 2026-07-08

## Advertising Principle

Anchor ads should describe preparation and communication support. Avoid claims that Anchor prevents harm, treats anxiety, replaces emergency help, diagnoses a condition, or guarantees safety.

Apple Ads offers placements across the App Store journey, including Today tab, Search tab, Search Results, and Product Pages. For v1, start with Search Results because users are already expressing intent through their search terms.

## Launch Sequence

### Phase 1: Store Page Ready

- Finalize App Store metadata.
- Publish Privacy Policy URL.
- Prepare screenshots with clear captions.
- Make sure the product page explains that Anchor is not a medical or emergency service.

### Phase 2: Small Search Results Test

Run a small Apple Ads Search Results campaign for 7 to 14 days.

Suggested structure:

- Campaign: Anchor - Search Intent - EN
- Ad Group 1: Support Cards
- Ad Group 2: Breathing / Grounding
- Ad Group 3: Journal / Reflection
- Ad Group 4: Safety Contact / QR

Start with conservative daily budget and bids until conversion data is available.

### Phase 3: Refine

After initial traffic:

- Pause keywords with low tap-through and no installs.
- Move converting search terms into exact match.
- Add negative keywords that imply treatment, diagnosis, emergency rescue, or unrelated anchors/boats.
- Test product page screenshots and subtitle.

## Safe Ad Copy Direction

Use:

- Prepare what others should know.
- Make a clear support card before you need it.
- Help others understand what you need during panic or overwhelm.
- Share simple instructions with a QR code.
- Keep a trusted contact close.
- Reflect privately after hard moments.

Avoid:

- Promise to stop symptoms.
- Treat anxiety.
- Stay safe in any emergency.
- Replace emergency services.
- Medical-grade support.
- Doctor-recommended unless actually documented.

## Metrics To Watch

- Impressions
- Tap-through rate
- Install conversion rate
- Average cost per tap
- Average cost per acquisition
- Search terms that convert
- Product page conversion rate
- Day 1 and Day 7 retention, if available later

## First Budget

For worldwide v1, start with a small English-language Apple Ads Search Results test before expanding.

Conservative starting idea:

- One campaign
- 4 ad groups
- Low daily budget, for example USD 10-25/day while learning
- Manual or conversion-maximizing bid strategy depending on available account data
- Review search terms every 2 to 3 days during the first two weeks

## Required Before Ads

- App must be live or approved for launch.
- Product page must clearly state the app's limitations.
- Privacy policy must be published.
- Screenshots must match the actual app.
- No medical/treatment claims in metadata or screenshots.
